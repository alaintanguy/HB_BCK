# HealthBridge Development Plan

## Vision

HealthBridge is an AI Caregiver platform that augments human caregivers
by combining communication, monitoring, documentation, and intelligent
clinical assistance into a single mobile workflow.

## Guiding Principles

-   Reduce caregiver workload
-   Improve patient safety
-   Improve documentation quality
-   Increase AI understanding of the patient's condition
-   Scale from one caregiver to many patients

## Development Rules

-   M1 (Motorola) = Development device
-   M2 (Samsung) = Stable demonstration device
-   One milestone at a time
-   Every milestone must compile and be tested
-   Commit after every completed milestone
